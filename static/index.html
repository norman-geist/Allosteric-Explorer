Open Methods and methodological references

Residue selection syntax

Simple IDs still work: A:40, PROA:158-170. A qualifier can identify a chain, segid, or chain/segid.

segid PROA and resid 40:60

(chain A or chain B) and not resname GLY

segid "PRO A" and (resid 40 or resid 85:95)

Supported fields: chain, segid, resid, resname, protein/all. Precedence: not → and → or. Quoted names and * / ? name wildcards are supported. These are residue-level selections; topology atom-selection fields use MDAnalysis syntax.

Structural ions and model scope

Recognized monatomic ions from the first reference PDB model are retained; recognition does not establish a structural or functional role. They can be shown as labeled spheres. Nearby ions are reported within 5 Å of pocket beads. Protein-only fpocket geometry does not account for ion occupancy. Communication and PRS remain Cα protein models; ion-mediated coupling is not explicitly calculated. Trajectory frames display only coordinates reconstructed from the mapped trajectory residues, so static reference ions are not overlaid on moving frames.
